This notice describes the personal Clipfarm Local installation and its use of YouTube API Services. The public information site is separate from the local processing application.

Information accessed and stored

After Google consent, Clipfarm stores OAuth tokens, the connected channel ID and name, upload session information, uploaded video IDs, publication status and available video statistics or Analytics reports. These records support channel verification, resumable uploads, scheduling, duplicate prevention and performance measurement.

The application also stores source URLs, footage, transcripts, rendered clips, generated metadata, review decisions, campaign receipts and operational logs. Optional campaign login sessions and Telegram bot configuration are stored only when the operator supplies them. Clipfarm does not request or store the operator’s Google password.

Use and sharing

Google receives account authorization, upload media, video metadata and API requests. OpenAI receives selected source-footage transcripts and sampled frames to propose moments and grounded metadata. OAuth client secrets and Google access/refresh tokens are not sent to OpenAI. Optional Telegram notifications may contain clip identifiers, status messages or published-video links.

Connected-account data is used to provide the operator’s publishing and reporting features. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models. Clipfarm Local’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Credentials and workflow records are stored on the operator’s PC in the dedicated local secrets directory and database. The installation binds its control services to localhost. The operator remains responsible for OS access, disk protection and backups.

Automatic media cleanup removes unreferenced working files after seven days and published renders after thirty days. Active jobs and unpublished renders are protected. Compact workflow records and collected reports are retained separately until removed by the operator; media cleanup does not erase the database. The operator should remove connected-account records when they are no longer needed.

Revoke access and request deletion

Revoke Clipfarm Local at Google Account connections to stop future authorized requests. Also pause the channel in local controls. To remove data already stored locally, ask the installation operator to remove that channel’s token file, account/Analytics records and associated media from the local database, disk and any backups. Revoking access alone does not delete local records or videos previously uploaded to YouTube. You can manage those videos in YouTube Studio.

For questions or deletion requests, contact the installation operator or the maintainer of this site’s hosting repository. This personal installation does not operate a separate hosted account database.

This website and other providers

This static site has no sign-in form, analytics scripts or advertising. Its GitHub Pages host may receive routine request information, such as IP address and browser information, under the GitHub privacy statement. Google’s handling of its services is described in the Google Privacy Policy. OpenAI and Telegram also operate under their own applicable provider terms and policies.

Changes

This notice will be updated before the application introduces new uses of connected-account data. Additional consent will be requested when the requested access changes.
